What your estimate is built from

It's a general estimate, not a quote. Here's exactly what goes into it, and what it can't know.

Roof area
Measured from satellite data, one plane at a time, including the parts you can’t see from the street.
Pitch
Measured for each plane. Steeper roofs take longer to work safely, so they cost more.
Local labor
Roofer wages in your metro area, from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
Materials
National installed costs, adjusted for this month’s material price index.
What it can’t see
Underlayment condition and damaged decking. A roofer confirms those on site.

Price My New Roof is an independent referral service, not a roofing contractor. If you ask for quotes, we pass your request to up to 3 licensed roofers near you, and they pay us for the referral. Seeing your estimate never shares your details.
